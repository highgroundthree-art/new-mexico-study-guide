What's on the New Mexico license exams

To get licensed, students have to pass three separate exams, each with a scaled score of 70 or better:

ExamFormat
NIC Esthetics Theory110 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes. 100 count toward the score and 10 are unscored trial questions.
NIC Esthetics PracticalHands-on with a mannequin: 6 core sections plus 2 New Mexico add-ons
New Mexico state lawMultiple choice on the Barbers and Cosmetologists Act and the Board's rules (16.34 NMAC)

Practical exam: how the points are weighted

Client prep and basic facial23%
Facial makeup22%
Blood exposure procedure20%
Eyebrow: soft wax and tweezing15%
New client prep and setup10%

Percentages are the NIC core weights as reported by iPREP. The first work-area setup makes up the rest. Blood exposure is worth one-fifth of the core points but takes only 10 minutes, so practicing it is time well spent. New Mexico also adds particle microdermabrasion on the forehead and full-band strip lashes on both eyes, 10 minutes each.
